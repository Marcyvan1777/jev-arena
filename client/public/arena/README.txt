ARENA PHOTOS
============
Put your own pictures here and they are hung on the arena posters automatically:

  ali.jpg     (also .jpeg, .png or .webp)   -> Muhammad Ali posters
  tyson.jpg   (also .jpeg, .png or .webp)   -> Mike Tyson posters

Portrait photos work best (the picture is cropped to fill a 2:3 frame). Refresh the page after adding a file.
No photos are shipped with the game: use images you have the right to use. Without them each poster shows a plaque
with the boxer's name, nickname and record.
